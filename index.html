About Us

Accessible Design Company has been in operation since July 2020. We are an international organization focused on ensuring that websites and software are accessible to individuals who require the use of assistive technology. We take pride in reviewing client applications for accessibility issues and offering solutions.

Services We Provide

We offer the following services to clients:

  • Review of website and/or application for accessibility issues
  • Implementation of accessibility attributes if needed
  • Confirmation that changes meet industry standard
  • One-on-One guidance to Independent Web Developers
